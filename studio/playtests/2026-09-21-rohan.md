## Findings

### 1. "Like copy link we need copy code button" — feature gap

The lobby has Copy link (through `WebClipboard`, fixed 18 Sep) but no way to copy the four-letter code
by itself. Friends on Discord will paste a code more readily than a URL, and the design's `#online`
rule 2 assumes the code is the thing that travels. → **T-0005**, light lane, small.

### 2. "some line of sight detection issues, maybe its the props or walls which have bigger collider than their visual" — presentation mismatch, rules probably right

The rules block sight on the **whole hex column**: a prop's `bodyHeight` (6) sits on the tile top and
the supercover ray test treats the entire hex as a prism (`#line-of-sight` rules 2 and 5). The
placeholder prop art is a **box narrower than the hex** (`PropView.BuildPlaceholder`: footprint is a
fraction of the tile's circumradius). So a shot that visually clears the corner of a pillar is refused,
because the rules say the pillar fills its hex. The player reads that as a collider bigger than the
visual, which is exactly what it is: the visual undersells the blocking volume.

Two things to check before deciding it is only visual: (a) whether the grazing rule ("blocked if either
flanking hex blocks") is producing refusals the player cannot predict from the picture, and (b) whether
heroes' bodies (also 6 units, also whole-hex) block shots that look open. Pillar 5 says a refused shot
must be explainable: the refusal already names the blocking hex; the client should show it.
→ **T-0006**, light lane: reproduce, then fix the art or the feedback, not the rule.

### 3. "should end turn automatically when all action points are consumed (maybe need to think about it based on full gameplay design)" — design question

Not a task. Logged as **OQ-N16** on `docs/design/mechanics.xlsx`. Arguments against auto-ending: a 0-AP
ability exists in the AP rules ("may be 0"), and the 21 Sep board mechanics add guaranteed actions and
builds the player may want a beat to look at. Argument for: the 30 s clock makes a dead turn cost real
time. Rohan wants it decided against the full gameplay design, not now.
