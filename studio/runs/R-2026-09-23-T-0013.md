## What is different now

A match now looks like the boards Rohan chose. Across the top, a thin track: your name and your round pips on
the left, theirs on the right, a marker on whoever's half it is; in the last seconds that half burns towards the
middle with an ember. Bottom left, your action points as a bolt, a number and eggs, then three captioned rows of
tiles — movement, weapon, spell — each showing its cost as dots, a violet diamond when a boon changed it, a
violet triangle when a Sigil added it, and **a small closed eye on every action your opponent has not seen you
use yet**; the eye goes the first time you use it and stays gone for the rest of the series. Your boons sit as
circles down the right edge. End Turn lights up when you have nothing left to spend. Arm an attack and the panel
over their head shows the damage, the rules' lines, and one "?" row for anything of theirs you haven't seen; a
blocked shot leads with why. Between rounds the draft is three ink cards painted in your gods' colours, and the
round card, the round's result and the series result sit in a dark band across the middle. The examine plate is
ink now too; the serif font is gone from the build. The board floats in black instead of the grey sky.

Behind it: one new piece of knowledge on the wire — your own view says which of your things the opponent has
seen (ADR-039) — tested in Core and over sockets; nothing about any rule changed.
