## What is different now

The camera is yours. Hold Q or E to turn round the arena, W A S D to slide across it (it stops at the
arena's edge, or further if you raise the margin), the wheel to zoom, Space to glide back to the start, and V
to flip between the side-on view — your hero always on the left now, even as seat 2 online — and a view from
behind your end looking at the opponent. Every speed, limit and angle is on the `ArenaCameraRig` component on
`Cameras/vcam_Tilted` in the Arena scene, and changing them in Play Mode takes effect immediately.
