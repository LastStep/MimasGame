## Findings the outline and the prep note did not have

1. **`Room` today has no Core `Session`; the rematch loop and the session are different loops.** ADR-032's
   room life is `Waiting → Playing → Waiting`. With a session it becomes
   `Waiting → Playing(round) → Drafting → Playing(round) → … → Waiting`. `Room.Round` (the rematch counter
   in `match.start`) and `Session.Round` (1..3 inside one series) are two different numbers and must not
   share a name on the wire.
2. **`SessionView.Match` is the round's `PlayerView`**, so a `session` block on every message can carry
   the view or the view can stay where it is. The outline's Q2 is really "is `view` nested or beside".
3. **`Room.Tick` reads `State.ActivePlayer` for the clock**; in a draft there is no active player, both
   seats have a deadline. The room clock needs a second mode (one deadline, two seats), not two clocks.
4. **`NetClient` remembers `CurrentMatchId` and treats a `match.start` after a reload as a reconnect.** A
   reload during a draft has no `match.start` to receive today; the resume path must carry the session.
5. **`RandomBot.ChooseDraft(Session, player)` exists**; the room only has to call it on the bot's seat
   when the phase is `Draft`, with the same think delay.
6. **The examine panel has no "Boons" list** (`HudExamine` has Items, Abilities, Modifiers); `UnitView.Boons`
   and `LineageId` are already in the view, so the client half is HUD only.
7. **`MatchSession.ShowResult` and `BackToLobby` assume the match's end is the way out.** With a session,
   a round's end leads to a draft or the next round; only `SessionEndedEvent` leads back to the room.
