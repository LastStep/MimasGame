## What is different now

Clicking a hero with nothing armed slides a **paper plate** in from the right, full height: the painting in
the lineage's hues with the emblem and the name in Cormorant, health and action points in light Josefin
numbers with a bar and eggs, the level the hero stands on, six stats as base then a green or red net, the
boons oldest to latest (the starting Blessing's circle filled), and the four items with their actions as
named tiles: violet-edged with a diamond when an Enchant changed a number, with a triangle when a Sigil added
the action. Resting on anything opens **one hover panel** to the left on ink: cost, damage in amber, reach
(violet when a boon changed it), apex, element as tiles; the ability's own sentence; the damage line as the
rules compute it (`3 base + 3 Strength − their armour`); what boons changed; the conditions. The enemy's
plate is the same in vermilion, with dashed `?` tiles for actions not yet seen, `?` rows for boons not yet
revealed, and a grey `?` after the four lane stats while one is hidden. A click anywhere off the plate only
closes it — it selects and arms nothing — and so do ✕ and Escape; the plate stays open across turns, and
End Turn and Resign step left while it is open. It works the same in the browser build, fonts and drawn
glyphs included. The rest of the HUD is unchanged except that the action bar is finally centred.

## Deviations from the plan

1. **The model reads a mirror rebuilt from the view, not `Rules`** (§7.5). In practice `Rules` is the truth,
   so §7.5 as written would have shown the bot's hidden Thor's Might as a green +1. Test-covered.
2. **Commits 5–7 are one commit** (`099c470`): the old view could not compile against the new model.
3. **A known net shows beside the grey `?`** ("3 +1 ?") rather than being replaced by it.
4. **`Glyph` is one element with a `shape` attribute**, not one class per glyph (§7.8).
5. **The icon key rides on an `icon--<key>` class**, because the element name carries the inventory id
   `ex.item[weapon].tile[0]` (§7.8 asked for the key in the name; §3's ids won).
6. **The inventory id is `ex.portrait`** (the book's), where §7.3 wrote `ex.painting`.
7. **The emblem is drawn in the light hue at 50%**, as the pinned render shows it, not at 6% (§7.3); noted
   as drift in `docs/ui/examine.md`.
8. **Captures go straight to `artifacts/t0011/shots/`** with `ScreenCapture.CaptureScreenshot`, not via
   `Assets/_Shots` (§11.5): native size, and no `.meta` churn.
9. **1280×720 does not scroll.** The HUD's panel settings scale with the screen (reference 1920×1080, match
   0.5), so every 16:9 window lays the plate out exactly as 1080 does. The scroll (and the 120px painting)
   happens on a short wide window; captured at 1920×520 in the browser. The spec assumed constant pixels.
10. **End Turn steps left at every size**, not only at 1280×720 (§13): the plate covers the corner always.
11. **The action bar is re-centred in code**, a pre-existing bug the plate made visible (commit `0e8c95c`).
    Out of the spec's scope, but without it End Turn landed on the bar.
12. **`tools/smoke/browser-smoke.mjs` holds its clicks 80 ms.** A tool fix, needed to click the board.
13. **The manifest did not change** with the lineage hues (it lists files).
14. **The first practice load from a playing Lobby spawns no units** — pre-existing, not chased.
